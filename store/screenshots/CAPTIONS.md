## Notes

- Hidden for the shots (transient pop-ups only): toasts, the "Day 1 streak" coin pop-up, and the
  first-run coach hints ("Drag the WALK pad…", "tap JUMP…"). Everything else is the normal HUD.
- 09 (airport): the Terminal 4½ hall — departures board, travellers with suitcases. Only the
  game's parody airlines (Kugel Air, El Oy, Shmear Jet, Nu? Air); no real airline names or logos.
  Scenes 01–08 were shot with `?test` defaults, where the airport stays off; nothing in them shows it.
- Friends (05): a real second player (a second game page joined to the same party through the
  local relay, `server/local-relay.js`) standing next to Lulu in Free Roam — the "2 riders" chip
  and the friend's name tag are the real party UI.

- Format: the script saves PNG; the files here were converted to JPEG (quality 90, no transparency — Apple
  accepts .jpg) to keep the repo small (97 MB → 20 MB). Exact pixel sizes are unchanged.
- Suggested upload: one orientation per device set. **Landscape** for iPhone (the game plays best sideways),
  **portrait** for iPad — or all portrait for a tidy store page. Order = the file numbers.
