Privacy Policy — Lulu's Road Trip

Last updated: 26 November 2026 (usernames and friends lists)

Lulu's Road Trip ("the game", "we") is a free game for iPhone, iPad and the web (lulu.boats). This page explains, in plain words, what information is involved when you play.

The short version

  • No email, no password, no real name. If you play online, you pick a made-up username for the game (we ask you not to use your real name). We never ask for your email, phone number or address.
  • Your progress (coins, cars, your house, your scrapbook) is saved on your own device.
  • The iPhone/iPad app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising identifier only if you tap "Allow" when the app asks about tracking.
  • If you play online, the game sends where your car or character is in the game to our game server, under a random player number and your username. Our server keeps your username, your friends list, friend requests, who you blocked and name reports. There is no chat.
  • The game never uses your real location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Saved on your device

Your game progress is stored in the game's own storage on your device. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data) deletes it. The game can show you a "save code" so you can back up your progress yourself; we don't keep a copy.

Playing online

Online play is on by default and can be turned off in the game's Settings ("Play online"). It is always off in Dina's mode. When it is on, the game sends our game server:

  • a random player number that the game makes on your device, with a secret only your device knows (our server keeps a scrambled copy of the secret to know it's you; the number is not linked to your device's advertising identifier, email or anything else about you);
  • your username: a made-up name you pick in the game, 3 to 16 letters and numbers. Every name is checked for rude words, pretending to be the game's staff, and phone numbers or social media. Please don't use your real name;
  • what is happening in the game: where your car or character is, which car you drive, which of the six preset emoji reactions you send, and your scores on the leaderboards (shown under a name picked from a fixed list of funny names);
  • if you add friends: your friends list, friend requests you sent or got, players you blocked, who may send you friend requests, and who may join you. Where you are in the game is only shared live with your friends and never stored;
  • if you report a player's name: that you reported it, and when (several reports replace the name);
  • if you own homes in the game: which in-game homes you own.

Other players see a see-through car or character labelled with your username (or a colour and a number, if they turned other players' names off). There is no text or voice chat and nothing you make or upload is shared. You can remove or block a friend, report a name, and choose who may send you friend requests at any time. Our server keeps your username, friends list, requests, blocks, name reports, leaderboard entries and home ownership so the game works; the rest is only passed on live and not kept. Like every internet service, the server sees your IP address while connected; we don't store it or use it to find you.

Advertising (iPhone and iPad app only)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To show and measure ads, the AdMob software may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), an app-specific device identifier, the ads you saw or tapped, app launches, approximate location worked out from your IP address, and crash and performance information about its own software.

On iPhone and iPad the app asks your permission before ads may track you (the App Tracking Transparency prompt). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The website (lulu.boats) shows no ads.

Game updates

The app can download newer versions of the game's own files from our server so fixes arrive sooner. These downloads send nothing about you; they only fetch game files.

Children

The game is rated 13+ on the App Store and is meant for a general audience. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children: there is no email or password, no chat, and the only thing a player types is a made-up username, which our server checks and filters (and asks not to be a real name). Players can block others, report names, and turn off friend requests or online play. Ads come from Google AdMob as described above. If you are a parent and have a question or concern, please contact us.

Your choices

  • Turn off online play in Settings ("Play online"), or hide other players' names (Settings → Your name).
  • Delete your online account in the game: Settings → Your name → Delete my online account. This deletes your username, friends list, friend requests, blocks and reports from our server right away.
  • Deny or withdraw tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete the app to delete everything saved on your device.
  • Ask us to delete leaderboard entries or home ownership linked to your player number — send us your save code (Scrapbook → Copy save code) and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy
