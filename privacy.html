Privacy Policy — Lulu's Road Trip

Last updated: June 2026

Lulu's Road Trip ("the app", "we") is a single-player casual game. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information is involved when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or any personal information ourselves. Your game progress (high scores, coins, stars, unlocked skins) is stored only on your own device.

Advertising

The app displays ads through Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To serve ads, AdMob may collect or receive information such as your device's advertising identifier, general usage data, and approximate, non-precise device information. This data is used to show and measure ads.

You can learn how Google uses this information here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

On iOS, the app will ask for your permission before allowing ads to use your advertising identifier for tracking (the App Tracking Transparency prompt). You can decline, and you can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Data retention & deletion

Because game progress is stored only on your device, deleting the app removes that data. We do not hold a copy on any server.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will reflect any changes.
