Support — Lulu's Road Trip

Something not working, a question, or an idea? Email us at info@veritybranding.com — tell us your device (e.g. iPhone 15, iPad Air) and what happened. We read everything.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Coins, cars and progress from earlier versions of the app carry over automatically. To move it to another device, use the save code in the game (Scrapbook → Copy save code).

I'm stuck somewhere in the world. Pause → "I'm stuck" puts Lulu back on the nearest road or pavement.

How do I play with a friend? Friends → add each other with a friend code or link, then join them in one tap. Online play can be turned off in Settings → "Play online".

Can I stop ads from tracking me? Yes — answer "Ask App Not to Track", or change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Where's the old 2D game? More → Classic 2D.
